# TWILIGHT 2.0 — Phase 2A

- Extended the existing side-scrolling town from 4,500 to 9,000 world units. The camera already follows the active player. This is a larger finite map, **not** an infinite or 3D open world.
- Distributed NPCs and additional cars along the extended map, and extended streetlights and trees.
- Each defeated civilian awards the attacking vampire a random 10–30 coins. Guards do not award coins.
- Every 10 total civilian hunts increases the vampire's level by one.
- Added level and coin counters to the in-game HUD.
- Coins and progression persist across rounds **within the current room/session only**. They do not yet persist after leaving the room or restarting the server.
- No weapon shop, human gameplay, new selectable locations, or 3D models are implemented in this incremental update.

Run: `npm start` from inside the `twilight` folder.
